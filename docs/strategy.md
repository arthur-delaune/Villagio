# Strategy: market, competition, model

## Market

- **Second-hand in France:** an estimated **€14bn in 2023** ([Republik Retail](https://www.republik-retail.fr/rse/seconde-main/pratiques/un-marche-de-la-seconde-main-estime-a-14-milliards-d-euros-en-2023.html)).
- **Pressure on family budgets:** **40% of parents** report strain from school-related costs ([CSA for Cofidis, 2024](https://csa.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2400489-Cofidis-Budget-des-parents.pdf)).
- **Target segment:** families with children aged 5–15 enrolled in sport, music or creative activities, where gear is outgrown before it wears out.

*Sizing the extracurricular-gear segment specifically is a hypothesis we are validating through our parent survey.*

## Competitive landscape

| Player | Strength | Gap Villagio addresses |
|---|---|---|
| **Vinted** | Massive reach, habit-forming for clothing | No activity-specific taxonomy, no club context, shipping-first |
| **Leboncoin** | Local, generalist, strong in equipment | No trust layer, no sizing help, hard to search for kids' gear |
| **Beebs** | Kids-focused second-hand | Covers kids' items in general (toys, clothing, childcare), not activities and clubs |
| **Decathlon second-hand** | Brand trust, retail footprint | Retailer-led and catalogue-driven, not community-led |

**Positioning:** the only marketplace organised around **where kids practise** rather than **what is being sold**.

## Business model

- **Transaction commission** split between buyer and seller, with integrated payments
- **Future revenue lines:** promoted listings, club subscriptions (dashboards, events), a premium tier for active families

## Go-to-market

1. **Club-led seeding.** Partner with a handful of clubs in one city. Each club is an acquisition channel and a liquidity pool at the same time.
2. **Seasonal spikes.** Concentrate effort on the end-of-season period (June) and back-to-school (September).
3. **Event loops.** In-person kit-swap days turn into digital listings and new sign-ups.
4. **Expansion.** Activity by activity, then city by city, once club density proves liquidity.

## Key risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Cold start (no listings, no buyers) | Seed club by club, use concierge matching early |
| Generalist platforms copy the features | The moat is the club network and community density, not the features |
| Low average order value | Activity packs and bundles raise basket size |
| Seasonality | Multi-activity coverage, spring and summer camps, music schools on different calendars |
